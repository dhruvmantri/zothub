What broke, and the rule that fixes it
Tracking must scale with size. −0.042em is right at 58px and unreadable at 29px. Rule: −0.042em above 44px, −0.038em at 28–44px, −0.02em below 28px.
Button pairs stack, they don’t shrink. Save + Apply side by side breaks the 44px target at 375px. Primary stays, secondary moves into an overflow.
The serif italic holds. It stays legible at 29px, which is the real test of the new signature.
Tab bar uses the accent as an inset rule, not a fill — a filled tab at this size swallows the label.

07 · Still openWhat I have not solved, stated plainly.

The wordmark. “zothub:” borrows Wellfound’s colon device. It needs replacing with something of our own before this is ownable.
Jade vs. the success state. If you pick Jade, the accent and “Accepted” are the same family and status legibility suffers. It would need a different success hue.
The serif is a system stand-in. Iowan/Charter/Georgia is standing in for a real italic face; the final pick happens at font selection.
