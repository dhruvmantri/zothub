zothubIcon exploration grounded in the brand · a nod to UCI

The mark, explored

You liked the stacked wordmark; these push on memorability and a light touch of UCI without impersonating it — no seal, no gold, no copy of Peter the Anteater. Each shown at avatar / app-icon / favicon / tab size, on dark and light, plus in the wordmark lockup. The italic hub and Pacific blue stay constant.

Honest read: the two words say the name in full, but at 16–32px they compress to a texture, not a form. Safe and legible large; least distinctive small. No UCI cue.

BThe RingRing Road · the hub at the centre

The idea: UCI is built as concentric circles — Ring Road around Aldrich Park. A ring with the hub at its centre means “hub” (our name, the gathering point) and UCI’s actual campus shape. Insiders read it instantly; outsiders just see a clean, ownable mark. Survives 16px as a solid form. Risk: a plain ring+dot can read generic (target/record) — the slight top gap (a “you-are-here” break) is what keeps it a place, not a bullseye.

CZotthe chant · a charged Z

The idea: “Zot!” is the UCI chant (the anteater’s zap). A single bold Z, leaned into the same italic as the signature, carries that energy — student, confident, unmistakably Zot. Cleanest read at 16px of the whole set. Risk: a lone Z is a common shape; the italic slant + the blue-on-disc are what keep it ours. (Could add a lightning cut if you want more edge.)

DThe Snoutanteater · abstracted, not Peter

The idea: the anteater is the most-loved UCI signifier; its long curled snout is the silhouette everyone knows. Drawn as one original bold curl (deliberately not the trademarked Peter), it reads “anteater = UCI” to anyone who knows, and as a friendly distinctive hook to anyone who doesn’t. Most ownable and most UCI. Risk: at 16px a curl can read as an abstract swoosh — needs the weight kept heavy.

EHub × Ringour own letter · the h from hub, in the campus ring

The idea: the icon becomes a piece of the wordmark itself — the italic h from hub (in accent) held inside the Ring (Ring Road). The one option that literally derives from our own mark and nods to UCI, so icon and wordmark are obviously the same brand. Risk: the h needs the ring’s breathing room, so it’s the softest of the set at 16px.

My lead: B (the Ring) or E (Hub × Ring) — the Ring Road nod is a genuine UCI insight that stays clean at every size and never risks looking like the official mascot, and E ties the icon straight to our own wordmark. D (the Snout) is the most emotionally “UCI” if you want the anteater in there; C (Zot) is the loud student pick. All keep the italic-hub wordmark beside them — the icon anchors app identity, the wordmark carries the name. Pick a direction and I’ll refine that one to final (and outline it to production SVG).
