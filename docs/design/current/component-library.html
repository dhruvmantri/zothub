05

Confirmation, keyed to severity

interactive

One confirmation model, weighted by stakes — not three paradigms applied at random. A reversible tidy-up asks lightly; an action that affects real people names the human cost and offers the gentler path first.

Low stakes — reversible

A quick confirm, dismissable, no alarm.

High stakes — affects real people

Names exactly who is affected; the safe path (“review first”) is the calm default, the destructive one is explicit.

The rejection a student sees — direct, then a door

Acknowledge first; offer second; never in the same breath. An auto-declined student reads the same words as a deliberately-declined one.

08

Avatars

Shape carries meaning: people are circles, clubs are rounded-squares — you know what you’re looking at before you read it, which matters on a two-sided marketplace where a review row is people and a Discover row is clubs. Fallback chain: photo → initials on a name-derived colour → a neutral glyph. Never a broken image.

Messages

interactive

Two relationships, one surface: student ↔ club (the officers running recruitment) and the net-new student ↔ member (Positioning §8 — ask someone actually in the club what it’s really like). Connection, not a feed. Person = circle, club = square, so you always know who’s on the other end. On desktop the list and thread sit side by side; on mobile it’s list → thread. The thread header is a link — tap the club to open its page, or a member to open their profile (never a dead end). Type in a composer and hit send.
