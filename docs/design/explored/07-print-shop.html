Direction 7 · The Print Shop

Campus silkscreen — the energy of posters stapled to a kiosk.

Unconventional on purpose: flat ink, hard borders, block shadows, poster type. Unmistakably student-made, impossible to confuse with an enterprise tool or an official campus system. Light is its native register — a poster is ink on paper. The dark theme inverts the press: the ground becomes ink, and the hard shadows become light, because a black shadow is invisible on black. Content illustrative.
