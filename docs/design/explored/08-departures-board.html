Wayfinding, not browsing — deadlines are departures.

Unconventional on purpose: the metaphor of transit signage, where colour codes the line, the board tells you what leaves next, and you always know how to get where you’re going. Amber = roles, green = events, cyan = clubs. Dark is its native register — a board is dark because the information is the light. The light theme prints the same signage system instead of illuminating it: route fills stay vivid, every colour used as text drops to an AA-safe value. Content illustrative.
